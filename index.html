01About

I'm an MSc Computer Science student at McGill University in Montreal. I'm drawn to data science, AI and machine learning, and I like turning algorithms into practical tools: from training deep neural networks to building LLM-powered apps.

Most recently I was an AI Engineering Intern (Mitacs) at Trebble FM, where I designed multimodal LLM pipelines for video editing, fine-tuned GPT-4.1 and Qwen3-32B, and built an LLM-as-judge evaluation that moved the team's focus from retraining to data quality. Before that, at Defect Scanner, I deployed real-time computer-vision SOP-adherence systems for manufacturers including Hyundai and Schneider Electric, and prototyped an LLM interface that turns plant operators' requests into code changes. I'm also a Graduate Teaching Assistant in McGill's School of Computer Science.

What I'm looking for: full-time AI/ML engineering and forward-deployed engineering roles from January 2027, where I can build GenAI and ML systems and work closely with the people who use them to get them into production.
